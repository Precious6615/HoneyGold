    About Us

    Reliable Roofing. Professional Contracting.

    Honey Gold Roofing (Honey The Gold Roofing Concept) is a roofing and general contracting company based at Federal Second Gate, Ijanikin, on the Badagry Expressway in Lagos.

    We supply and install high quality aluminium long span, roofing sheets, flat and cladding sheets, stone-coated metro tiles and roofing accessories. Beyond the roof, we take on general contracting work, so you can plan your building project with one dependable team.

    Our approach is simple: listen carefully, explain your options clearly, and deliver neat, careful work you can be proud of.
